# Royal Service Domestic Workers Center Homepage Redesign

## 1. Website Overview

Royal Service Domestic Workers Center, operating under the Tad-Beer UAE brand, provides domestic worker recruitment, placement, visa assistance and related administrative services in the UAE.

The homepage targets families, parents, household employers and UAE residents looking for maids, nannies, babysitters, housekeepers and caregivers. The redesign focuses on communicating the full range of services, improving service discovery and presenting recruitment and visa information more clearly.

## 2. Main Improvements

### 1. Clearer Homepage Positioning
- Improved the presentation of the centre's core services, including domestic worker recruitment, placement and visa assistance.
- Made the overall service offering easier to understand from the beginning of the homepage.
- Strengthened the communication of the centre's combined recruitment and administrative support.

### 2. Improved Service Discovery
- Organized maid, nanny, babysitter, housekeeper and caregiver services into clearer categories.
- Improved service navigation to help visitors identify suitable household staffing solutions.
- Made service information easier to explore based on individual household requirements.

### 3. Clearer Recruitment and Visa Information
- Improved the presentation of recruitment packages, visa services and processing information.
- Addressed inconsistencies between fixed processing timeframes and arrangements that depend on individual circumstances.
- Structured service expectations more clearly to help customers understand the recruitment and administrative process.

## 3. Design Improvements

- **Visual Presentation:** Refined the homepage to establish a professional appearance appropriate for a domestic worker services centre.
- **Layout:** Improved content hierarchy and organization across recruitment, household staffing and visa-related services.
- **User Experience:** Made service categories and customer enquiry options easier to find.
- **Consistency:** Improved the presentation of service descriptions, pricing information and processing details.
- **Overall Appearance:** Created a more structured homepage that communicates the centre's services and customer support more effectively.

## 4. Project Summary

The Royal Service Domestic Workers Center homepage redesign addresses three key issues: unclear business positioning, difficult service discovery and inconsistent recruitment and visa information. The updated structure helps UAE households understand available services, explore suitable staffing options and take the next step towards making an enquiry.
