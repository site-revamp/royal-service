About Royal Service

Helping households make informed choices.

Royal Service Domestic Workers Center, presented as Tad-Beer UAE, supports households looking for domestic worker services in the UAE. From exploring suitable service options to understanding related documentation, the team can guide you through the next steps.

Whether you are looking for a maid, nanny, babysitter, housekeeper or caregiver, start with a conversation about the support your household needs.
